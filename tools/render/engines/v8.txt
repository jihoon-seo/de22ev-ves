# v8: cross-plane V8, uneven burble.
#
# Based on upstream example1.esc (DasEtwas/enginesound e5fcca5, MIT; 10 cylinders, big piston
# term, low ignition, damped straight pipe). Changes vs example1:
#  - 8 cylinders, firing order 1-8-4-3-6-5-7-2 at even 90 deg (cross-plane crank); odd
#    cylinders on the left bank, even on the right. Per bank the pulses come at 270/180/90/180
#    deg (left: 1 3 5 7 at 0/270/450/540): with a separate pipe length per bank the summed train
#    is uneven, which gives the burble (cycle orders 1..7 around the 8th-order note)
#  - left bank extractors ~0.55 ms shorter than the right bank (example1: ten different
#    lengths); a larger difference burbles more but lets sub-orders beat the firing note
#  - primaries/intakes: example1's first eight cylinders
#  - ignition-dominated cylinder pressure (ignition 4, piston 2) and less intake (0.12)
#  - some crankshaft fluctuation (example1: 0) for a loping idle
#  - throttle section (ves.render extension) for the overrun sound
