# i6: inline-6, smooth rising wail.
#
# Based on upstream example2.esc (DasEtwas/enginesound e5fcca5, MIT; 10 cylinders, low exhaust
# reflection, strong ignition, short pipes) with the straight pipe and muffler of example5.esc
# (same repo, MIT). Changes:
#  - 6 cylinders, firing order 1-5-3-6-2-4 at even 120 deg
#  - equal-length 6-into-1 extractors (example2: ten different lengths): almost only firing
#    harmonics, so the note stays smooth and its harmonics sweep up through the pipe
#    resonances as the rpm rises (the wail)
#  - primaries/intakes: example2's first six cylinders
#  - less intake (0.12) and engine vibration (0.02) in the mix
#  - less crankshaft fluctuation (example2: 0.216) for a turbine-smooth idle
#  - throttle section (ves.render extension) for the overrun sound
